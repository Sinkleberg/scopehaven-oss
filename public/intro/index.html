IBM 2025 / INDUSTRY CONTEXT

US$670,000

Higher average breach costs

In IBM's 2025 study of 600 breached organizations, high shadow AI use was associated with higher average costs than low or no use. Shadow AI means AI used without organizational approval.

An observed comparison, not total global AI losses or measured ScopeHaven savings.

Enter the local workbench ↗
What can I do today?+

Run core demo, response and framework checks, inspect findings and backend evidence, add investigation notes, compare compatible runs, and export evidence. The owned staging and local-model run is a separate CLI workflow.

What does a repair mean?+

A failed check becomes a verified repair only when a compatible candidate passes it and all required permitted-use controls pass. Selecting the repaired fixture does not patch another application.

What is outside this build?+

Customer and production assessments, general-purpose external scanning, security certification and compliance conclusions remain outside the current release.
